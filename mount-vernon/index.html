Keeping Your Mount Vernon Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year if their water is hard.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms like rusty hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a surprise cleanup.

Hot Water Headaches Mount Vernon Homeowners Face

Well before a tank floods a Mount Vernon garage, it usually sends signals. Look out for a popping or rumbling sound while the tank heats, rust-colored hot water, a faint rotten-egg smell, a damp spot under the tank, or hot water that runs out a little sooner each month. These are early signs of heavy sediment, a depleted anode, a weak element or burner, and early corrosion, and when they're caught early, they're usually an affordable repair or a planned replacement. Left alone, they end in a ruptured tank and a cleanup bill. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it means the tank is seeing too much pressure or temperature, and it needs a professional look soon.
