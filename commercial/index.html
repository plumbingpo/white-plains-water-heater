Food Service Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a rush, and a tank with too little recovery falls behind right when the dish room backs up. White Plains Water Heater specializes in food-service hot water, including high-recovery gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing based on peak-hour demand, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. Our technicians verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and log every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Replacements and maintenance visits are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install overnight or before opening, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Heavy-Use Systems

Hot Water for Hotels, Apartments, and Large Facilities

Hotels, multifamily buildings, and large facilities run their hot water around the clock, and the equipment has to keep up. The morning shower rush can drain an undersized system fast. Our technicians service banks of commercial tanks, racked tankless units, and recirculation loops built for nonstop demand, with a structured plan for each property.

For a large facility, downtime gets measured in refunds and tenant complaints, so we plan for prevention first. That means comprehensive system assessments, sizing based on real occupancy, multiple heaters piped in parallel so one failed unit never leaves the building cold, recirculation pump and valve service, and routine flushing and descaling to hold capacity. If a unit does go down, our water heater repair technicians isolate it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast for emergency repairs anywhere in White Plains and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be booked overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. White Plains Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses on a regular schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and more consistent water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
